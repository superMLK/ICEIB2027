% G: General
Eating out is part of daily life for most adults in Taiwan. A 2025 survey of
1{,}000 office workers aged 22--65 found that nearly 70\% eat out regularly and
more than 40\% eat out every day~\cite{tnf2025survey}, while about three in ten
adults aged 20 or older meet the criteria for metabolic
syndrome~\cite{hpa2026metabolic}. Worldwide, 1.4 billion people had
hypertension in 2024, and only about one in five had it under
control~\cite{who2025hypertension}. At the same time, more people simply ask a
chatbot whether a meal box is suitable for them, which makes large language
models (LLMs) a new entry point for dietary advice.

% H: However
Fluent answers, however, are not correct numbers. When five chatbots evaluated
ready-to-eat meal boxes from Taiwanese convenience stores, all of them
underestimated sodium, and repeated queries varied with a coefficient of
variation of 20--70\%~\cite{hsuan2025mealbox}. Renal diets generated by
ChatGPT, Copilot, and Gemini were all below the energy reference, some exceeded
the safe potassium limit, and none fully met the
guidelines~\cite{aydincil2026renal}. When an LLM ranks candidates, its output
also depends on their order in the prompt~\cite{hou2024llmrank}. For a person
with hypertension or chronic kidney disease, such errors become health risks.

% I + J: In literature and judgement
Existing approaches leave this gap open. Diet-logging apps record meals after
the fact. Health-aware recommenders treat health as a soft objective that can
be traded for accuracy~\cite{zhang2025mopihfrs,vasantlal2026morla2c} and
mostly recommend recipes rather than meals one can buy nearby. Wearable energy
estimates are often used as ground truth, although their errors exceed
30\%~\cite{germini2022wearable}. What is missing is an eating-out agent in
which deterministic rules own safety and numbers, the LLM owns understanding and
expression, live activity data and walkable meals are combined, and safety
holds even when the LLM fails.

% K: Keypoint and contributions
This paper proposes NutriAgent, a grounded conversational agent that integrates
clinical nutrition rules, wearable IoT data, and Google Gemini. A deterministic
gate runs before any LLM call, so the LLM only sees a safe candidate pool and
safety comes from the architecture rather than from prompts. The main
contributions are as follows.
\begin{itemize}
    \item A grounded agent architecture in which rules own safety and numbers
    and the LLM owns language.
    \item An auditable rule base covering the AHA/ACC, ADA, ACR, and KDIGO
    guidelines and Taiwan's 11 mandatory allergen categories.
    \item A numeric verification and fallback mechanism for LLM replies, with
    grounding and fallback rates as metrics.
    \item An evaluation design with five baselines and a working prototype with
    a preliminary functional check.
\end{itemize}
