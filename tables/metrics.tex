    \centering
    \caption{Evaluation Metrics and Targets}
    \label{tab:metrics}
    \footnotesize
    \setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
    \begin{tabular}{@{}p{1.75cm}p{4.65cm}p{1.45cm}@{}}
        \toprule
        Metric & Definition & Target \\
        \midrule
        CVR & recommendations violating a clinical rule / all recommendations
            & 0\% (with or without LLM) \\
        AVR & recommendations containing a declared allergen / all
            & 0\% \\
        Grounding rate & numbers in the reply that match the database, measured
            for raw Gemini output and for the final reply & 100\% (final) \\
        Intent accuracy & correct intent and context (place, time, activity,
            allergen) on the labeled dialogue set & $\ge 90$\% \\
        Calorie MAE & $\frac{1}{N}\sum |\text{kcal} - B_t|$ over recommendations
            & $\le 180$\,kcal \\
        Latency & offline engine per request; end-to-end with Gemini (P50/P95)
            & $\le 50$\,ms; P95 $\le 5$\,s \\
        Fallback rate & replies falling back to the template (timeout, error,
            mismatch) & reported \\
        Position consistency & top-3 overlap over five shuffled orders of the
            same candidates & reported \\
        Convergence & interactions until top-1 matches a changed preference
            & $\le 5$ \\
        User study & adoption rate, SUS, perceived clarity (5-point Likert)
            & SUS $\ge 68$ \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
